# Creignificent Automation

Business automation systems built to support real operational workflows for **Creignificent LLC**.

This repository documents practical automation work for proposals, lead tracking, customer follow-up, reporting, and business process improvement.

## Purpose

The goal of Creignificent Automation is to reduce repetitive administrative work, improve follow-up consistency, and turn everyday business processes into reliable systems.

## Focus Areas

- Lead intake and lead tracking
- Proposal generation
- Customer follow-up workflows
- CRM-style status tracking
- Google Workspace automation
- Operational reporting
- Business process standardization
- AI-assisted workflow experiments

## Design Principles

- Keep workflows understandable and maintainable
- Automate repetitive work without removing human oversight
- Minimize unnecessary data collection
- Keep credentials and secrets out of source code
- Use clear logs and status tracking where automation performs business actions
- Prefer low-cost, practical technology that can be maintained by a small business

## Portfolio Context

This repository is part of a broader portfolio focused on the intersection of:

**Artificial Intelligence + Cybersecurity + Business Operations**

The emphasis is on building systems that solve real business problems rather than creating demonstrations with fake operational data.

## Security

Automation involving customer information, email, files, or external services should use least-privilege access and secure credential storage.

See `SECURITY.md` for the repository security baseline.
